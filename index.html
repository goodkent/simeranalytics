How we work

Four steps, and you can see the work the whole way through.

STEP 01

A conversation

Thirty minutes, no deck. You tell us what's frustrating; we tell you whether we're the right people. Sometimes the answer is no, and we'll say so.

STEP 02

The audit

We get read access, follow the data, talk to the people using it. You get a plain-English roadmap with effort and cost against each item.

STEP 03

The build

Two-week slices, a short demo at the end of each, and a standing invite to your channel. Priorities can change between slices; nothing is locked in a contract you regret.

STEP 04

Keeping it alive

Alerts, documentation, and a walkthrough with whoever inherits it. We'd rather you didn't need us than have you stuck with us.

Who you'd be working with

Small on purpose

Simer Analytics came out of twenty-eight years in utilities — budgets and rate cases, meter data management, forecasting, planning and GIS, with a compliance deadline always somewhere on the horizon. Long enough to watch good analysis die because nobody could get the pipeline to run, and working pipelines sit unused because nobody made them easy to look at.

So we kept the team small and the range wide. The person who scopes your project is the person who writes the queries, builds the pipeline and ships the app. You won't get handed to a junior after the sales call, because there isn't a sales call.

That background means you won't spend the first month explaining your industry to us. It also means we've built the ordinary things — dashboards, pipelines, internal tools, integrations — for people who answer to a regulator, and for plenty who don't.
